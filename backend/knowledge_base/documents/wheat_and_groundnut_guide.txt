# ICAR Advisory: Wheat Irrigation Critical Stages and Groundnut Nutrient Management
# Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) & Directorate of Groundnut Research (ICAR-DGR)

## 1. Wheat (गहू / गेहूं) Cultivation & Irrigation Scheduling
Wheat is the premier Rabi cereal crop. Optimal sowing date for Central and Western zones is November 1 to November 15.
Late sowing beyond November 25 causes significant yield reduction due to terminal heat stress during grain filling.

Critical Irrigation Stages (Ranked by Water Sensitivity):
1. Crown Root Initiation (CRI Stage - 20 to 25 days after sowing): THE MOST CRITICAL STAGE. Missing irrigation here reduces tillering by up to 30%.
2. Tillering Stage (40 to 45 DAS).
3. Late Jointing Stage (60 to 65 DAS).
4. Flowering Stage (80 to 85 DAS).
5. Milk Stage (100 to 105 DAS).
6. Dough Stage (115 to 120 DAS).

Rust Disease Management:
- For Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis) and Brown Rust: Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1 ml/liter of water (500 ml in 500 L water/ha) immediately on seeing pustules.

## 2. Groundnut (मूंगफली / भुईमूग) Soil & Calcium Requirements
Groundnut requires light-textured, well-drained sandy loam, red sandy, or light alluvial soils with pH 6.0 to 7.0.
Heavy black clay soils cause pod rotting and make harvesting extremely difficult.

Key Nutrient Requirement - Gypsum Application:
- Groundnut pods absorb calcium directly from surrounding soil rather than through roots.
- Calcium deficiency leads to "Pops" (empty shells without kernels).
- Apply Gypsum @ 400 to 500 kg/ha at 40 to 45 days after sowing (flowering to early pegging stage).
- Broadcast gypsum around the plant base and incorporate lightly into the soil followed by irrigation.

## 3. Onion (कांदा / प्याज) Purple Blotch Disease Control
Purple Blotch (जांभळा करपा) caused by Alternaria porri:
- Symptoms: Small, sunken, whitish lesions with purple centers on foliage and seed stalks.
- Chemical Control: Foliar spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 grams/liter of water or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/liter of water mixed with sticker (spreader) @ 0.5 ml/L.
