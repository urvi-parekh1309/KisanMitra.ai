# ICAR-CICR Cotton Advisory: Pink Bollworm and Sucking Pest Management
# Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) - Central Institute for Cotton Research

## 1. Sowing Window and Soil Requirements
Cotton (कापूस / कपास) thrives best in deep black soils (Regur soil) with good drainage and a pH range of 6.5 to 8.0.
Sowing should be carried out upon the onset of monsoon rains once the soil receives at least 75 to 100 mm of cumulative rainfall.
Optimal sowing time across Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Central India is between June 15 and July 15.
Avoid late sowing beyond July 20 as it increases vulnerability to bollworm attacks during the reproductive phase.

## 2. Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) Symptoms & Life Cycle
Pink bollworm is one of the most destructive pests affecting Bt and non-Bt cotton.
Symptoms:
1. Rosetted flowers ("गुलाबी बोंडअळीच्या प्रादुर्भावामुळे फुले कमळासारखी मिटलेली / चोथा झालेली दिसतात"): The larvae web the flower petals together.
2. Premature boll opening and staining of lint.
3. Internal shedding of young squares (पात्या) and small bolls.
4. Dropped squares and yellowing of surrounding foliage.

## 3. Economic Threshold Level (ETL)
Monitoring must begin 45 days after sowing using pheromone traps.
- Install 8 to 10 sleeve pheromone traps per acre with Gossyplure lures.
- ETL: If 8 moths per trap per night are captured for 3 consecutive nights, or if 10% damaged green bolls with larvae are found, immediate control measures must be initiated.

## 4. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Recommendations
Phase 1 (Preventive & Biological - 40 to 60 Days):
- Erect yellow sticky traps (10/acre) for whiteflies and jassids.
- Release Trichogramma bactrae egg parasitoids @ 60,000 eggs/acre at weekly intervals (3 releases).
- Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 5 ml per liter of water.

Phase 2 (Chemical Management at ETL - 60 to 90 Days):
- First spray: Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 ml/liter of water or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/liter of water.
- In case of severe infestation of mature bolls: Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 5 grams per 10 liters of water (0.5 g/L).
- Always use protective gear and avoid spraying during high wind or mid-day heat.
