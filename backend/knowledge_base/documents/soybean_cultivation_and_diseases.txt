# ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research: Crop Advisory & Yellow Mosaic Management
# Directorate of Soybean Research (ICAR-IISR), Indore

## 1. Soil and Climate Suitability
Soybean (सोयाबीन) requires well-drained, fertile medium to deep black clay soils with high organic matter.
Optimal soil pH is 6.5 to 7.5. Sandy or waterlogged soils are strictly unsuitable.
Soybean is a Kharif crop requiring 600 to 750 mm of well-distributed rainfall.

## 2. Sowing Guidelines
- Recommended sowing period: June 20 to July 10 (after receiving at least 100 mm monsoon rainfall).
- Seed treatment: Treat seeds with Trichoderma viride @ 5-10 g/kg seed or Thiram + Carbendazim (2:1) @ 3 g/kg seed.
- Bio-fertilizer inoculation: Inoculate with Rhizobium japonicum and Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 5 g/kg seed for biological nitrogen fixation.
- Row spacing: 45 cm between rows and 5 to 7 cm between plants. Seed rate is 65 to 70 kg/ha for 70% germination.

## 3. Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV) Symptoms & Identification
Yellow Mosaic Virus (सोयाबीनचा पिवळा मोझॅक रोग / पीला मोजेक वायरस) is a devastating viral disease.
Symptoms:
1. Young leaves develop irregular bright yellow patches scattered across green leaf tissue.
2. Progressive chlorosis turning entire leaves completely golden yellow.
3. Infected plants remain stunted with few, underdeveloped, or empty pods.
4. Transmission: YMV is not transmitted through seed or soil; it is transmitted exclusively by the Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) insect vector.

## 4. Yellow Mosaic Virus Management Strategy
Because YMV is viral, there is no direct chemical cure once a plant is infected. Management relies strictly on vector control:
1. Rogue out and bury severely infected plants early (first 30 days) to prevent field spread.
2. Install 15 yellow sticky traps per hectare to monitor and trap whitefly vectors.
3. Chemical vector control at early sign of whiteflies:
   - Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 4 grams per 10 liters of water (100 g/ha).
   - Or spray Betacyfluthrin + Imidacloprid @ 350 ml/ha mixed in 500 liters of water.
   - Repeat spray after 15 days if vector population persists.
